    How to play

    Goal

    Get your marks in a row — across, down, or diagonally. On the 3×3 board you need three in a row; on 5×5 you need four. X always goes first.

    Your turn

    Tap any empty square to place your mark. If every square fills up with no line, it’s a draw.

    Modes

    Pick a 3×3 or 5×5 board, then play the computer (Easy, Medium, Hard), a friend on this device, or a friend online. On the 3×3 board Hard plays perfectly — the best you can do is a draw.

    Controls

    • 1–9 place on that square
    • Ctrl+Z undo
    • N new round
