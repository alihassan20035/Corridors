How to play

Goal

Red starts at the bottom and wins by reaching any square on the top row. Blue starts at the top and wins on the bottom row.

Your turn

Do one of two things: move your pawn one square up, down, left or right, or place one of your ten walls.

Meeting face to face

If the other pawn is right next to you, jump straight over it. If a wall or the edge is behind it, step diagonally to either side of it instead.

Walls

A wall is two squares long and slides into the grooves between squares, horizontally or vertically. Walls can’t overlap or cross, and you can never cut off a player’s last route to their finish line.

Controls

  • M move mode, H / V wall modes
  • Space or right-click flips the wall
  • Enter places, Esc cancels a preview
  • Ctrl+Z undo

On a phone, tap a groove to preview a wall, then tap Place wall.
